Omega AI: Fleet Trial

An Onslaught and a Hammerhead lead two Wolf frigates against a mixed enemy fleet.
This mission provides fixed ships and map objectives for comparing fleet behavior.

Enable Omega AI defaults to Yes in LunaLib.
For the first test, enable only Omega AI, LunaLib, and LazyLib, then restart the game.
Give your fleet Search & Destroy so capture and escort assignments do not suspend Omega.
Use autopilot if you want Omega to control the flagship too.
Logging is optional and starts disabled.

Small action labels show the movement decision each Omega pilot is executing:
Advancing, Engaging, Pursuing, Retreating, Waiting for support, or Disengaging.
Retreating means backing out of local danger, not leaving the battle.
Existing fleet orders and manual control take precedence.

This build controls targets, range, facing, approach, and withdrawal for ordinary combat ships.
Weapons, missiles, shields, venting, systems, and emergency collision avoidance use native game modules.
Carriers, fighters, phase ships, and custom ship pilots retain their existing control.
The upper-left HUD shows active counts or the reason for missing control.
AI Tweaks and RTSAssist ship controllers remain unsupported.
Set Enable Omega AI to No to restore previous pilots. Combat effectiveness still needs in-game validation.
