Omega AI: Fleet Trial

An Onslaught and a Hammerhead lead two Wolf frigates against a mixed enemy fleet.
This mission provides fixed ships and map objectives for comparing fleet behavior.

Before the first run, select Observe in LunaLib's Omega AI configuration.
Enable Log decisions and Combat status.
Use autopilot for the flagship and let the battle proceed without orders.
Observe mode records proposals without changing combat decisions.

Repeat the mission with Coordinate selected and the same configuration.
Watch whether the frigates reach combat before support, whether ships chase without closing, and whether regrouping delays useful attacks.
Record losses, battle duration, and any repeated waiting or movement loops.
Compare several runs because battle outcomes vary.

This alpha changes only limited fleet coordination.
Missile use, fighter missions, shields, venting, and individual ship piloting retain their existing behavior.
