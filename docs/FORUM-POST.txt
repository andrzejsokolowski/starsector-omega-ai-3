[b]Current scope[/b]
Omega AI starts with fleet coordination: helping unsupported ships wait for heavier support, rejoining idle stragglers, and breaking pursuits without useful closing progress.
This is an early alpha. The broader work on survival, focus fire, exploitation, missiles, and carrier wings is still pending.
An improvement in actual battle outcomes is not yet established.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Starsector 0.98a-RC8, LunaLib 2.0.0 or later, and LunaLib's required libraries.

[b]Try the alpha[/b]
Install the release ZIP through your mod manager, or extract its OmegaAI folder into Starsector's mods directory.
Omega AI starts in Observe mode, which records proposed decisions without issuing orders.
Open LunaLib, select Omega AI, and enable diagnostic logging.
Run the Omega AI: Fleet Trial mission with flagship autopilot enabled.
Repeat with Coordinate selected to test temporary regrouping orders.

[b]Control and compatibility[/b]
Existing player and commander orders take precedence.
Carriers, phase ships, fighters, stations, and unknown custom ship controllers retain their existing control.
Earlier Omega AI mods and active AI Tweaks fleet cohesion block coordination.
For a baseline comparison, disable other combat behavior mods.
